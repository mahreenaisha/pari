# 🎭 Pari Live Pitch & Demonstration Playbook

A structured cheat sheet for live demonstrations and pitching to judges.

---

### Act 1: Ambient Udhar Entry (Natural Shop Vernacular)
* **Phrase to Speak**:
  > *"Gupta ji ne 350 ka samaan liya, 200 UPI diya, 150 baaki."*
* **Pari Speaks Back**:
  > *"Gupta ji, total ₹350, ₹200 mile, ₹150 baaki. Save karoon?"*
* **Merchant**: *"Haan"*
* **Pari Prompts Payment Link**:
  > *"Save ho gaya. ₹150 ka payment link bhejun?"*
* **Merchant**: *"Haan, 98765 43210"*
* **Pari Confirms**: *"98765 43210. Sahi hai?"* $\rightarrow$ say *"Haan"* $\rightarrow$ Pari dispatches Paytm SMS link.

#### Vernacular Math & Fractions:
> *"Sharma ji ka saade teen sau ka bill tha, dedh sau diye, do sau udhaar."*
* Pari extracts *saade teen sau* ($350$) and *dedh sau* ($150$) effortlessly.

---

### Act 2: The "Soundbox Killer" (Ambient Counter QR Settlement)
1. In the app, long-press the QR code on the **QR** tab (or press <kbd>Q</kbd> in CLI).
2. **Pari Proactively Asks Aloud**:
   > *"₹150 aaya, Gupta ji ka?"*
3. **Merchant Answers**: *"Haan"*
4. **Pari Settles Entry**:
   > *"Theek hai, Gupta ji ke khaate mein jod diya. Khaata band."*

---

### Act 3: Counter Cash Repayment & Math Calculations
* **Phrase to Speak**:
  > *"Verma ji ne 100 diye."*
* **Pari Computes against Ledger**:
  > *"Verma ji se ₹100 mile. ₹300 baaki tha, ab ₹200 bachega. Save karoon?"*
* **Merchant**: *"Haan"* $\rightarrow$ Pari: *"Save ho gaya. Verma ji ka ab ₹200 baaki."*

#### Overpayment / Change:
* Say: *"Gupta ji ne do sau rupaye diye"* (when ₹150 owed)
* **Pari**: *"Gupta ji ne ₹200 diye, par udhaar ₹150 hi tha. Khaata band ho jayega, ₹50 wapas dene hain. Save karoon?"*

---

### Act 4: Natural Language Voice Ledger Queries
* Customer inquiry:
  > *"Gupta ji ka kitna baaki hai?"* $\rightarrow$ *"Gupta ji ka ₹150 baaki hai."*
* Shop summary inquiry:
  > *"Kul kitna udhaar hai?"* $\rightarrow$ *"Kul ₹1,200 baaki hai, 3 khaaton mein."*

---

### Act 5: Conversational Guardrails & Disambiguation
* **Mid-Readback Correction**: When asked to save, say: *"Nahi, 250 diye"* or *"Haan, lekin 250 diye"* $\rightarrow$ Pari recalculates instantly.
* **Missing Details**: Say *"200 ka saamaan diya"* $\rightarrow$ Pari asks: *"Kiske naam likhoon?"*
* **Customer Disambiguation**: Say *"Gupta didi ne 200 ka samaan liya"* $\rightarrow$ Pari asks: *"Gupta didi aur Gupta ji ek hi hain?"*

---

### Act 6: 11 Indic Languages
* Switch language via the chip on the Listen screen (e.g. Tamil, Bengali, Telugu).
* UI strings, screen read-backs, and Bulbul TTS switch to authentic regional female voice personas.
